Latch

With latch on, a full open flips the vocoder on and it stays on until you make a fist. Good for filming, since your hand can leave the frame.

Serve this locally so the browser grants camera and mic access: python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/. Headphones required. Raise mic gain until the band ladder moves clearly on speech, then set drive so consonants cut through.
